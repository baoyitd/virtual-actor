# 01. Product Context

## Product Positioning

`virtual-actor` is positioned as an **enterprise digital role asset operations platform**.

It is not just a role configuration tool. The product is intended to help a group enterprise turn expert experience, role responsibilities, business knowledge, boundaries, and AI capability into:

1. governable assets
2. publishable assets
3. reusable assets
4. observable assets
5. integrable assets

## Product Value

The product aims to solve several practical problems in enterprise AI transformation:

1. Expert knowledge and role know-how are scattered across people, documents, and temporary workflows.
2. Many “AI roles” exist only as prompt fragments or project-specific configurations, not as managed enterprise assets.
3. Enterprises lack a stable way to create, test, publish, use, observe, and evolve role assets.
4. Business users need usable role assets in real tasks, but current tools often stop at management or configuration.
5. Traditional backend-style configuration creates high adoption friction for non-technical users.

## Product Logic

The core product chain is:

```text
AI creation -> asset governance -> test & publish -> unified consumption -> operational evidence
```

This chain is the core product logic. The redesign should strengthen this chain, not weaken it.

## Target Users

The product serves more than one user type:

1. platform owner / AI governance team
2. role creator / operator
3. tester / reviewer
4. business consumer
5. downstream product or system consumer

## Design Direction

The new UI/UX should make the product feel like:

1. a serious enterprise product
2. business-oriented rather than technical-first
3. AI-native rather than “traditional form system plus a few AI buttons”
4. suitable for Chinese enterprise users

The visual tone should be:

1. steady
2. professional
3. business-like
4. clear for long-time desktop usage
