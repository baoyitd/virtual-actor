# 03. Core User Flows

## Flow 1: AI-Assisted Role Creation

User:
role creator / operator

Goal:
describe a needed business role in natural language, let AI produce a draft, then confirm and continue editing.

What this flow must support:

1. choosing AI-assisted creation
2. describing intent in business language
3. seeing AI-generated draft content
4. understanding which fields are AI-generated or AI-recommended
5. confirming, editing, and saving the draft

## Flow 2: Manual Role Configuration

User:
role creator / operator

Goal:
manually configure a role as a governed enterprise asset.

What this flow must support:

1. understanding each field without external explanation
2. filling role identity, point of view, boundaries, business domain, and related settings
3. configuring business output in a business-friendly way
4. avoiding overwhelming backend-like form burden

## Flow 3: Knowledge Binding

User:
role creator / operator

Goal:
bind relevant knowledge to a role in a way that remains understandable when there are many files and folders.

What this flow must support:

1. browsing available knowledge
2. understanding knowledge scope
3. selecting at scale
4. batch selection by structure where appropriate
5. seeing saved selections clearly after reopening

## Flow 4: Role Testing

User:
tester / reviewer / role owner

Goal:
test a role before publish and understand whether it is good enough to release.

What this flow must support:

1. sending a test query
2. seeing the response clearly
3. understanding status and boundary signals
4. seeing structured business output in readable form
5. seeing sources
6. recording review / score / history

## Flow 5: Formal Role Usage

User:
business consumer

Goal:
use a published role for a real task and quickly understand the answer and structured result.

What this flow must support:

1. clear distinction from the testing experience
2. confidence that this is the formal usage path
3. readable result structure
4. understandable status and boundary signals
5. clear next-step meaning for non-success cases

## Flow 6: Marketplace Discovery

User:
business consumer

Goal:
find the right role asset by business scene or task intent.

What this flow must support:

1. discovering roles by scenario, not only by list browsing
2. AI recommendation or guided discovery
3. enough information to trust a role before trying it
4. trying a role quickly
5. understanding what each role is good for

## Flow 7: Operational Review

User:
platform owner / operator / governance team

Goal:
understand whether role assets are being created, published, used, blocked, or underperforming.

What this flow must support:

1. a quick operational picture
2. meaningful metrics instead of raw counts only
3. insight into creation, usage, quality, and risk
4. decisions about what to improve next
