# 06. Expected Design Deliverables

The design partner does not need to follow a rigid document template, but the output should at least cover the following content.

## Required Output Areas

1. current-state design audit
2. redesign principles
3. information architecture proposal
4. key page designs
5. key flow designs
6. visual system direction
7. content / copy / field guidance strategy
8. implementation slicing suggestion

## Minimum Questions The Design Should Answer

The design package should clearly answer:

1. Why will users find it easier to complete key tasks?
2. Why does the new design fit Chinese enterprise usage better?
3. Why does the new design better express the product as a role asset operations platform?
4. What should be redesigned first, and what can wait?

## Recommended Key Surfaces To Design

1. login
2. global layout and navigation
3. role asset list
4. role detail
5. role create/edit
6. knowledge binding
7. test desk
8. usage desk
9. marketplace
10. dashboard

## Design Expectations

The new design should show:

1. strong business clarity
2. reduced form burden
3. AI-native interaction thinking
4. clearer trust and decision support for business users
5. a more complete and more convincing product identity
